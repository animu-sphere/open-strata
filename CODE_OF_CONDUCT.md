# Code of Conduct

OpenStrata welcomes people with different backgrounds, experience levels, and
perspectives. Participate with respect, assume good intent, and make room for
others to contribute.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive and considerate in issues, pull requests, discussions, and
  other project spaces.
- Focus feedback on ideas and work, not people.
- Respect differing viewpoints and personal boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory or degrading language, personal attacks,
deliberate disruption, and sharing someone else's private information are not
welcome here.

## Reporting

Repository administrators accept content reports. To report a concern, email
**piggypenguin583@gmail.com**. Please include enough context for us to
understand what happened. Reports are handled privately where possible.

Administrators will review a report, may ask follow-up questions, and may take
action ranging from a reminder to removal of content or participation. We aim to
acknowledge reports within a few business days.

This Code of Conduct applies in all OpenStrata project spaces and when someone
is representing the project publicly. Security vulnerabilities belong in the
private process described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
